How Casanova Study handles student data

Casanova Study uses AI to grade student work and to build study materials, which means some student information is sent to an AI service. This page says exactly what is sent, what is not, who else touches it and how it is protected.

A one-page summary for teachers and school leadersNovember 2026Full policy: casanovastudy.com/privacy

What the AI sees, and what it doesn't

→Sent to the AI (Anthropic), only to do the job

  • Grading: the photo or scan of the student's paper (including the name written on it), the teacher's answer key, and the class list so it can match names to papers.
  • Studying: the topic, notes or photos a student uploads, and the questions they ask the study helper.

×Never sent to the AI

  • Student names for class summaries and reteach plans: these are written from scores, with students numbered, not named.
  • Student names for personalized practice: the AI is told which topics need work, never who.
  • Birth dates, emails, passwords, parent contact details, or grades from any other class.

How the AI provider is limited

  • We use Anthropic (the company behind Claude) through its business API, not the public chat app.
  • Under Anthropic's commercial terms and data processing addendum, content we send is not used to train its AI models.
  • Anthropic processes the content only to return the result to us, and may use it for nothing else.

Who runs the site

  • Supabase (database and file storage), Vercel (hosting), Anthropic (AI) and Resend (email), plus Google when a student or teacher signs in with a school Google account or imports a Google Classroom roster.
  • Each has a data processing agreement on file and may use the data only to provide its service to us. Scans and uploads live in private storage, never on a public link.
  • We never show ads, sell or rent student information, or build advertising profiles.

Who can see what

  • Grades stay with the teacher until released. The teacher reviews, and can change, the AI's marks first.
  • Students' own studying is private. Teachers see results only on practice they assigned, not everything a student studies.
  • Only verified teachers (a school email) can connect to student accounts in a class.
  • School leaders who use the school overview see counts per teacher, never student names.

Laws we follow

  • FERPA With schools we act as a "school official" under the school's direction, using student information only to provide the service.
  • COPPA Students under 13 need a parent's approval, or the school's when it authorizes the app for classroom use. Accounts stay locked until then.
  • Florida No selling, no targeted ads, no profiles for non-school purposes (s. 1006.1494). Breach notice to the school within 72 hours of confirming it; Florida law allows 30 days.
