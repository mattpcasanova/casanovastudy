Student data and ground rules
Full policy: casanovastudy.com/privacy

The AI sees

  • The paper, your answer key, your class list (to match names).
  • What a student uploads or asks the help tutor.
  • Anthropic's business API: nothing we send trains its models.

It never sees

  • Names in class reports or reteach plans (students are numbered).
  • Birth dates, emails, passwords, parent details.
  • A student's other studying. You see only the practice you assigned; administrators see counts, never names.

Please do

  • Sign in with the school Google account, students too.
  • Review before you release. Check any "Check this" flag.
  • Lay pages flat when photographing; names are read from page 1.
  • Keep the tab open while a set grades.

Please don't

  • No IEPs, 504 plans, medical or discipline records. Coursework only.
  • Don't share your account.
  • Don't post practice links publicly; share in class or on Classroom.
  • Not for placement or promotion decisions on its own.

Questions teachers ask

Does it work for my subject?Anything with an answer key: multiple choice, short answer, free response, math work, essays with a rubric. It knows AP, AICE, SAT, ACT and EOC formats.
No answer key?It drafts one from the first paper for you to edit. Your own key is better.
How accurate is it?98 of 100 marks kept on the Somerset reports teachers checked. When unsure, it flags instead of guessing. You are the grader.
Can students cheat with the tutor?You choose per assignment: hints only (default), off, or full. You can see what each student asked.
How do scores reach Focus?Class results → Send to gradebook → Focus. Matched on student ID.
What does it cost?Nothing during the pilot.
Help?Find Matt, or email hello@casanovastudy.com. For one paper, press Regrade on its report with a note.
