Authorization of Casanova Study for classroom use (pilot)

To whom it may concern,

Somerset Academy Charter High School, Pembroke Pines, Florida (the "School") authorizes Casanova Digital LLC ("Provider") to make its Casanova Study service available to the School's teachers and students for classroom and homework use during a pilot from November 9, 2026 through January 22, 2027 (the "Pilot"). The Provider offers the Pilot at no cost to the School.

  1. Purpose and use. Teachers may use the service to grade student work, review class results, and assign study materials and practice. Students may use it to study and complete work their teachers assign. The Provider will use student information only to provide the service to the School and for no other purpose, and will not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.
  2. FERPA. For the Pilot, the School designates the Provider a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (34 CFR 99.31(a)(1)). The Provider is under the School's direct control with respect to the use and maintenance of education records, will use them only for the purposes authorized here, and will not redisclose them except as the School directs or the law requires.
  3. Students under 13. Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and the Federal Trade Commission's guidance on schools, the School consents on behalf of parents to the Provider's collection of personal information from students under 13, solely for the educational purposes described in this letter. The Provider's privacy policy (casanovastudy.com/privacy) and its one-page student data summary describe what is collected and how it is used. The School will make these available to parents on request.
  4. Security and incidents. The Provider maintains a written information security program and will notify the School within 72 hours of confirming any unauthorized access to the School's student information, and will cooperate with the School on any notice to families.
  5. Data return and deletion. At the School's request at any time, and in any case within 30 days after the Pilot ends unless the parties agree to continue, the Provider will export the School's student data to the School on request and then delete it, except where a teacher's own account retains work product the School directs the Provider to keep.
  6. Term. Either party may end the Pilot on written notice. The School's data privacy agreement, if any, controls over this letter where the two differ. Continuation after the Pilot, and any fees, require a separate written agreement.
